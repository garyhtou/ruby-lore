Ruby on Rails did not start life as a framework. In 2004, [[dhh]] pulled it out of the code of **Basecamp**, a real product at 37signals, and released the reusable parts. Then in 2005 came the demo that changed everything: a screencast showing how to [[fifteen-minute-blog]], which spread like wildfire and pulled a generation of programmers toward Ruby. Much of Ruby's modern popularity traces back to that extraction and that video. ([rubyonrails.org](https://rubyonrails.org/))

Ruby reached its first public **1.0** release on **December 25, 1996** (build 1.0-961225), a few years after [[matz]] settled on the name and started building the language ([[named-before-it-existed]]). It was Ruby's very first Christmas-Day ship, the seed of what later grew into [[the-christmas-release-tradition]].

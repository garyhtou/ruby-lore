Ruby 3.0 shipped on **December 25, 2020**, delivering on [[matz]]'s long-stated "Ruby 3x3" goal of making Ruby three times faster than Ruby 2.0, alongside new concurrency tools like Ractor. Like the releases before it, it landed as part of [[the-christmas-release-tradition]].

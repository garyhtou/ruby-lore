Ruby's first public release was version **0.95**, distributed on **December 21, 1995** to `fj.sources`, one of the Japanese Usenet newsgroups (the "fj" hierarchy stands for "from Japan"). This was the language's public birth, roughly two years after [[matz]] settled on the name and started building it ([[named-before-it-existed]]).

The "0.95" is just a version number below 1.0, marking it as an early, pre-1.0 build rather than a finished one. The first release Matz numbered **1.0** followed a year later, on Christmas Day 1996 ([[ruby-1-0]]).

**Chunky bacon** is a phrase the cartoon foxes shout throughout [[why-the-lucky-stiff]]'s *Why's (Poignant) Guide to Ruby*. It means nothing, which is exactly the point: it is an affectionate inside joke that signals you know a little Ruby folklore. This very site lives at chunkybacon.dev because of it. If you want the story behind the phrase, start with the [[why-the-lucky-stiff]] page.

When Rubyists say **"the Pickaxe,"** they mean **Programming Ruby**, the book by Dave Thomas and Andrew Hunt that introduced most of the English-speaking world to Ruby around 2000 (some accounts say 2001). The cover has a pickaxe on it, so the nickname stuck. For years it was *the* reference, and the name is still shorthand for "the canonical Ruby book." ([Pragmatic Programmers](https://pragprog.com/titles/ruby5/programming-ruby-3-3-5th-edition/))

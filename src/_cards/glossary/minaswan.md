**MINASWAN** stands for **"Matz is nice and so we are nice."** It is the closest thing Ruby has to a golden rule: because the language's creator, [[matz]], is famously kind and welcoming, the community aspires to be the same. You will see it invoked to keep discussions friendly and to welcome beginners. It is a big part of why people describe Ruby as the friendliest corner of programming. ([ruby-lang community pages](https://www.ruby-lang.org/en/community/))

*Omakase* is the Japanese phrase for trusting the sushi chef to pick your meal. [[dhh]] used it in a 2012 essay to describe Rails' philosophy: rather than make you assemble every component yourself, Rails serves a curated, opinionated default menu chosen by people who have cooked this before. You can still swap dishes, but the default is a considered whole. It is one of the most-quoted metaphors in the Ruby world.

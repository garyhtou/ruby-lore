This is Ruby's design philosophy in five words. [[matz]] built Ruby to make the *person writing the code* comfortable and productive, even when that costs the computer some efficiency. It is why Ruby often reads almost like English and why there is usually a pleasant way to do a thing. When Rubyists explain why they love the language, this is almost always underneath the answer.
